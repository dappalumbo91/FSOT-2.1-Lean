We evaluate the Fluid Spacetime Omni-Theory (FSOT) seed-locked scalar engine against the full public IAU Minor Planet Center orbital catalog (MPCORB). Using the uniform prediction law measured × (1 + |S(domain)| × factor) at the catalog's dimensional interface, we report a pooled median residual of approximately 0.023% across ~1.55 million minor-planet objects — well inside the repository green gate of 0.5% and inside the 0.05% aspiration band. No per-object free parameters are fitted. The catalog panel is wired into the multi-domain scientific atlas and multi-prover obligation export. We document the refinement process (D_eff, utilization, POOF/observer stack) and provide one-command reproduction from the public GitHub repository. This domain paper supports — but does not by itself constitute — the broader FSOT Theory of Everything claim developed in the flagship checklist paper.
