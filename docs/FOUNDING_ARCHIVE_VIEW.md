## 1. The medium is a river, not a stage

Standard physics treats spacetime as a rigid stage that things happen *on*. He does not.

Spacetime is a **compressible fluid**. Mass is debris in the current. Gravity is the fluid condensing around that debris — and, at high pressure, *seeping* and *pushing*. Dark matter is not a new species of particle. It is a **thread** in the same medium: thicken the fluid and dwarf cores soften, rotation curves flatten, clustering changes. Dark energy is not a static Λ poured in from outside. It is the fluid’s late-time stretch — a flow feature, not a separate substance.

The water-droplet picture from 2.0 is the official intuition hook: surface tension is the baseline, a pebble is a ripple, the subsurface couples every scale. Still water is ΛCDM. The river is FSOT.

That is why “a real theory can’t cherry-pick.” If the medium is one, you do not get to keep Hubble in cosmology, Casimir in the lab, and consciousness in philosophy as if they were different ontologies.

## 2. Seepage and push — two faces of one current

Gravity is two-faced.

- **Seepage:** higher-D curvature leaks into 4D. That is infall, condensation, the “inhale.”
- **Push:** the same fluid has a repulsive face. Collapse is not destiny. Expansion, bubble sectors, and white-hole outflow are the “exhale.”

The 6.1 tunnel thesis made this a cycle, not a metaphor: compress → tunnel → expel → suck. A black hole is a condenser, not a trash can. A white hole is the same information coming back out. The Big Bang, in that picture, is a white-hole expulsion. Pair them and you have a fridge-cycle: high-pressure liquid on one side, low-pressure gas on the other.

FSOT 2.1 keeps that cycle as **C2** (BH→WH valve) and **C3** (bubble bleed). Hubble tools disagree because they sit in different *sectors* of the same fluid, not because there are two universes.
