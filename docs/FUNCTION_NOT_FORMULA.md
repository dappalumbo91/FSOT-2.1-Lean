## Why “not dependent on the original formula” is load-bearing

Each millenium write-up picked a *representation*:

- NSE as a PDE on \(\mathbb{R}^3\) with a smoothness yes/no.
- BSD as equality of two integers cooked from Weierstrass models and \(L\)-functions.
- Hodge as a statement about \((p,p)\) classes on every projective manifold.
- YM as constructive QFT axioms.
- Riemann as a statement about every zero of a particular meromorphic function.
- P vs NP as a statement about two complexity classes.

Those representations are useful. They are not sacred. Several of them are
**already known to be the wrong zoom** for the function they name:

1. **Mean cascade is not \(L^\infty\) existence.** Kolmogorov \(4/5\) and Onsager \(1/3\)
   are \(L^2\) flux. Clay asked \(L^\infty\) smoothness. Stuffing \(4/5\) into existence
   is using the original formula’s *neighbor* as if it were the question.
2. **Raw \(L(1)\) magnitude is not rank.** 17a1’s \(L(1)\approx 0.387\) looks like a
   rank-3 seed if you treat the first-of-rank ladder as a lookup table — that *is*
   depending on a flawed encoding. The function is vanishing order plus Sha volume.
3. **An unnamed Hodge class has no residual.** Hunting Hassett \(C_{48}\) is enumerating
   the original moduli formula. Seeds attach to **named varieties**. No name, no Gram.
4. **Lattice glueball \(m/\sqrt{\sigma}\) is not the gap question.** Teper is a check of
   the flux-tube orifice. The question is why free color is not in the spectrum.
5. **Euler \(\mu=0\) is not viscous NSE.** A \(16^3\) Euler run that drifts energy is
   under-resolution, not a blow-up theorem, and not the Clay object.

FSOT’s move is APPLY: name the orifice, split the zooms, hit the table that
exists. Native objects (POOF, SUCTION, \(D_{\mathrm{particle}}\), \(\varphi\), process time)
are the *same* engine used on CRC sound speeds and LMFDB Sha. They are not a
retune of Clay’s manuscript.

## Per-problem: asked → function → FSOT simulation

### Navier–Stokes

**Asked.** Given smooth, divergence-free, finite-energy data on \(\mathbb{R}^3\),
does the solution stay smooth?

**Function.** 3D fluids stretch vorticity and dissipate through viscosity. Labs
publish inertial-range flux, wall \(\kappa\), sound speeds, scale height, ice/water
tables. DNS of viscous Taylor–Green decays at accessible Re. Nobody publishes a
percent error on “is NSE smooth?”

**FSOT.** Two zooms of one orifice: Particle-floor stretching
(\(4/5=1-1/D_{\mathrm{particle}}\)) and Fluid-tank viscosity (\(\mu(D)>0\), dark).
2D has no stretching (\(3/2\) at \(d=2\), not \(D_{\mathrm{particle}}\)).
Seed-locked 3D spectral Taylor–Green on \(\mathbb{T}^3\): energy \(0.125\to 8\times 10^{-7}\),
\(\max|\omega|\) \(2\to 0.005\), stretching is 3D. Euler \(\mu=0\) on the same grid
is the wrong orifice (energy drifts \(1.48\%\), not a singularity theorem).
Cartoon \(\omega_0\) scan vs Riccati threshold **6/6** — a valve sketch, not Clay.

**Depiction.** People plot \(E(k)\sim k^{-5/3}\) and vorticity isosurfaces.
FSOT plots energy/\(\max|\omega|\) vs time at seed \(\mu\), and the \(4/5\) identity
as a number against Kolmogorov, not as a smoothness certificate.

### Birch–Swinnerton-Dyer

**Asked.** Rank of \(E(\mathbb{Q})\) equals order of vanishing of \(L(E,s)\) at \(s=1\),
and the leading coefficient equals the arithmetic volume.

**Function.** Named elliptic curves have published \(\Omega\), Reg, Tam, torsion,
special values, and Mordell–Weil generators. The volume
\(\mathrm{Sha}_{\mathrm{an}}=L^{(r)}(1)/r!\cdot|E_{\mathrm{tors}}|^2/(\Omega\cdot\mathrm{Reg}\cdot\prod c_p)\)
is **1** on those curves. Integer rank for every \(E\) is not a residual.

**FSOT.** First-of-rank *scale* on 11a1 / 37a1 / 389a1 / 5077a1 / 234446a1
(seed leadings, green or aspiration). General rank is **vanishing, not magnitude**:
17a1, 53a1, 643a1, 11197a1, 501029.a1, 19047851.a1 plus out-of-sample twins all
give \(\mathrm{Sha}_{\mathrm{an}}=1\) (**17/17**). Modularity (Wiles–BCDT) produces \(L\)
without a seed lookup. Kolyvagin is the proven first rank\(=\)ord \(L\) theorem for
\(r=0,1\). \(r\ge 2\) is already vanishing+Sha — naming Kato next is enumeration.
11a1 torsion \(=5=D_{\mathrm{particle}}\) is an observed Mazur point, not an \(L\) lookup.

**Depiction.** People plot \(L(E,s)\) near \(s=1\) and watch the order of the zero.
FSOT plots that schematic *and* the Sha panel vs 1. Nearest-template of raw
specials is shown as the misfire the original encoding invites.
